# Reform's 2023–2027 programme

Research checked on 19 October 2026. The content covers Kaja Kallas's government after the 2023 election and the continuation under Kristen Michal. Contemporary policy is a source of concrete projects, while TFR's world events determine which allies and institutions remain available. No focus assumes that the historical invasion of Ukraine, NATO accessions or European appointments necessarily happened unchanged.

The principal framework is the government's [2023–2027 agreement](https://www.valitsus.ee/en/coalition-agreement-2023-2027), supplemented by enacted measures and the [2024–2027 agreement](https://www.valitsus.ee/en/coalition-agreement-2024-2027). Policies attributed to other coalition parties are presented as government policy; the mod does not claim they were exclusively Reform proposals. The actual coalition remains the one formed from the party ledger. Eesti 200 and Social Democrat participation is not forced.

## Entry, timing and priorities

- A valid 2023 election with Reform as the largest party grants the mandate. The historical pre-election Kallas tree does not grant it by itself. The programme becomes selectable under a confirmed Reform-led cabinet supported by at least 51 coalition seats.
- Thirty-two focuses at 35 days each require **1,120 days**. Starting on 5 March 2023 gives 29 March 2026; starting on the historical cabinet date of 17 April gives 11 May 2026, **235 days before 2027**. These are uninterrupted focus-time calculations, not a guarantee against coalition collapse, delayed cabinet confirmation or defeat.
- One common root opens five parallel columns of six focuses. The report requires all other 31 policies. Individual policies have ordinary prerequisites and no calendar gates.
- Defence funding and industrial-site planning are available immediately after the root. The two factory projects are the second and third nodes of the industrial column: their earliest completion is after **105 and 140 total focus-days**, respectively. Air defence and the Baltic line are available within the first 140 and 175 focus-days on their column. Important preparation does not require the final report or completion of civilian branches.
- The tree does not close simply because a European war starts early. Occupation suspends independent content, and persistent policy flags preserve completed reforms for supported restoration.
- Kallas's replacement by Michal is a scripted change within the same electoral mandate. It neither clears these policy records nor provides a second set of rewards.

## Script and balance rules

All money amounts and numerical modifiers are balancing choices. They are not converted historical appropriations. Costs use the existing Estonia spending helper and TFR's inflation-adjusted debt tooltip; revenue measures use the parent's inflation-adjusted income helper. Public investment therefore has a visible ledger cost rather than appearing as a free building.

The ammunition and drone projects each have a persistent pending flag and a distinct granted flag. They check states **13, 191, 1329 and 840**, in a defined order, and only build in a state **owned and controlled by Estonia with a free unlocked military-factory slot**. There are no extra free slots. If every eligible slot is occupied or territory is unavailable, the paid entitlement waits; the daily programme tick retries it. A factory is marked delivered only after its building effect runs. Completing either project again, refreshing the tree or handing over the premiership cannot grant a second copy.

The new energy measures share five OR-combined preparation slots with the Ratas and earlier Kallas programmes. Grid works cover slots 1–2, wind permitting covers slot 3, and renovation covers slots 4–5. An old and new project filling the same slot count once. All three new measures can finish Oil Crash recovery if earlier governments funded none, and already removed spirits are not reintroduced.

Permanent economic and military bonuses use the existing dynamic modifiers and exact native delta previews. Every focus reward is guarded by its own persistent completion record. Existing focus descriptions are outside this generator and remain unchanged.

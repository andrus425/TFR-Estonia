# Retained Ratas government: continuation sources

Sources checked on 15 October 2026. The continued Ratas premiership is alternate
history. Policy commitments and pandemic planning below are contemporary source
material, rather than a claim that this government historically lasted to 2023.

links the [Centre–EKRE–Isamaa agreement, signed on 8 April 2019](https://valitsus.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2021-06/50_Valitsuse%20programm%202019-2023_Ratas.pdf).

parties' election programmes. Avoid repeating the opening's second-pillar bill,
EUR 7 pension increase, highway opening, border section, excise cut and research
budget commitment.

## Pandemic measures available to Ratas II

The [Social Ministry's December 2020 health-system plan for 2021](https://www.sm.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2022-05/tervisesusteemi_plaan_covid-19_kriisi_uletamiseks_22.12.2020_0.pdf)
provides contemporary material for these additional focus subjects:

| Proposed policy subject | Plan section; printed page |
|---|---|
| Testing and contact-tracing capacity | 2.2–2.3; 4–5 |
| Hospital isolation wards, oxygen and staff | 2.4–2.5; 6–8 |

The plan distinguishes active preparations from future investment. County
hospitals and family-doctor access also connect these measures to chapter 7
of the coalition agreement. These pandemic responses should require actual
COVID cases or a response plan already under way, rather than a global news
announcement alone.

The [14 December 2020 vaccination plan](https://www.sm.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2022-05/covid-19_vaktsineerimise_plaan_14.12.2020.pdf),
sections 2–4, supports a further **risk-group vaccination through family doctors**
focus. It covers priority groups, hospital/family-doctor/care-home delivery,
European joint procurement, transport and the cold chain. This was a plan
prepared under Ratas II, so a continuation can draw on it without borrowing a
later cabinet's policy programme. Its implementation in TFR remains authored
alternate history.

## Adaptation and timing constraints

The suggested pool totals **18 focuses**: fifteen agreement subjects and three
additional pandemic subjects. All lasting 35 days would require 630 days. With
an opening completed during January 2021, that allows completion during autumn
2022, before the regular March 2023 election. Actual timing must also be tested
against the implementation's conditions and parent crisis hooks.

Arrange policies into several short branches with alternatives between them.
Reveal the continuation only after the player chooses to retain Ratas. A focus
lasting 35 days can approve funding, establish a programme or start procurement;
it should not claim to finish an LNG terminal or continental grid conversion
within that period.

TFR's energy crisis is an authored setting. Its relief focuses adapt the
agreement's energy and tax commitments; the 2019 document did not predict TFR's
crisis. Regional infrastructure funding can prepare Estonia in advance. Relief
for an active crisis must use the installed parent's crisis status, while
avoiding changes that end the crisis for other countries or remove unrelated
economic modifiers.

Rewards, fiscal costs, focus order, polling effects, coalition cohesion and
crisis mitigation are game-design choices. The sources do not establish those
numbers. Prefer the existing economy/military dynamic modifiers and explicit
budget effects to adding another set of permanent national spirits.

# Implementation contracts

## Ownership and data flow

The `TFR_EST_` prefix is reserved for this submod. Additive files separate the
parliament, shared systems, normal content and occupation. The parent installation
is read-only; exact-path overrides are generated and audited separately.

The compatibility manifest hashes the exact upstream and generated bytes. Git
attributes fix LF line endings for the generated overrides. The generator retains
the upstream BOM and records raw upstream hashes separately from canonical-LF
output hashes; checkout therefore preserves reproducibility.

Party IDs are stable: Reform 1, Centre 2, EKRE 3, Isamaa 4, SDE 5, Eesti 200 6,
Parempoolsed 7. Suffixes are `reform`, `centre`, `ekre`, `isamaa`, `sde`, `e200`, `pp`.
Each has support (integer basis points), seats, active, coalition and supply
variables. Polling plus other votes totals 10,000; seats total 101 after every
valid election. Invalid inputs leave the previous assembly in place and log a
diagnostic. `TFR_EST_pm_party` is confirmed office; `TFR_EST_nominee_party` is a
automatically selected cabinet leader. Confidence support and actual cabinet
seats are separate. `TFR_EST_mandate_party` fixes coalition leadership: Centre
at the start, then the party with the most seats after each successful election.
Strict comparisons use the existing party order for exact seat ties. Polling,
confidence loss, negotiations and AI preferences cannot change the mandate.

An election selects the mandated party as nominee and a mandatory coalition
member, then marks `TFR_EST_electoral_handover`. The incumbent remains caretaker
until 51 votes confirm that successor. Confirmation requires nominee=mandate
and either nominee=incumbent or an authorized electoral/scripted handover.
Confirmation consumes the handover flag. Between elections, ordinary confidence
recovery can reconfirm only the same incumbent.

The seven former nomination decisions are removed. Their effects are guarded
script-only hooks for future authored events/focuses. An explicit exception uses:

```text
set_country_flag = TFR_EST_scripted_cabinet_change
TFR_EST_nominate_reform = yes
```

The nomination consumes that one-use authorization, updates the mandated party,
and marks `TFR_EST_scripted_handover`; the new cabinet still needs 51 votes.
No ordinary player decision or AI controller sets this authorization. The Porto
Franco story explicitly uses it for a retained Centre cabinet or Reform successor.

`TFR_EST_snap_election` is a separate one-use story API. An event/focus must set
`TFR_EST_scripted_snap_election` immediately before calling it. The flag is
consumed even on rejected polling/occupation/restoration inputs. A successful
vote costs three cohesion; regular and restoration scheduling bypass this API.
There is no player snap decision or automatic caretaker vote.

Weekly repair seeds a missing mandate from the saved incumbent rather than the
largest historical delegation, preserving the opening Centre exception. It
restores mandatory leader membership and normalizes the nominee. Occupation
preserves this data; restoration retains it until its scheduled new election.

The native country leader is the constitutional president, matching Germany's
presentation in TFR. Kaljulaid and Karis each have roles covering all five normal
government ideology groups. The PM ledger controls a locked `head_minister` idea
and `head_of_gov_dynamic_loc`, independently of the president's native portrait.
Prime-minister labels use `TFR_EST_pm_name`, rather than `Root.GetLeader`.

`TFR_EST_reconcile_executive_display` is idempotent: it promotes a president only
when the active native leader is wrong, removes stale PM ideas, and installs only
the current PM. It grants no extra policy bonuses. Suspension removes those
ideas and the shared GUI flag without replacing the occupation administrator.
Restoration reuses the saved president flag and PM party; caretakers retain the
incumbent. The shared TFR GUI and diplomacy interface remain inherited. One
generated scripted-localization override appends seven ledger selectors while
preserving all 139 upstream selectors.

The parliament owns the election calendar. Native elections are disabled.
The first regular election is March 2023; weekly scheduling produces one election
within its window. A restoration election supersedes a same-week regular election.
Polling campaigns preserve totals through bounded transfers and never change seats.

## Public effects

- `TFR_EST_initialize_country_modifiers`: fill only missing economy/military
  variables with zero and repair the two permanent dynamic modifiers.
- `TFR_EST_refresh_country_modifiers`: install missing containers and immediately
  refresh their variable-driven effects after an authored adjustment.
- `TFR_EST_initialize_systems`: guarded first initialization, called after parent startup.
- `TFR_EST_initialize_parliament`: seed the historical assembly and Ratas cabinet.
- `TFR_EST_parliament_tick`: weekly elections, formation, confidence and executive display repair.
- `TFR_EST_assign_election_mandate`: select the largest party after valid seat commit.
- `TFR_EST_repair_leadership_mandate`: preserve the stored leader and required membership.
- `TFR_EST_suspend_parliament` / `TFR_EST_restore_parliament`: occupation and restoration.
- `TFR_EST_reconcile_executive_display`: constitutional president and TFR head-minister presentation.
- `TFR_EST_change_cohesion = yes` and the matching readiness effect:
  consume `TFR_EST_cohesion_delta` / `TFR_EST_readiness_delta` temporary inputs,
  clamp canonical meters to 0–100 and refresh derived national spirits.
- `TFR_EST_spend_budget = yes`: consumes temporary `TFR_EST_budget_cost` in
  millions of euros and converts it to the inflation-adjusted TFR debt ledger.
- `TFR_EST_refresh_programme`: reconcile only the currently confirmed programme.
- `TFR_EST_mobilize_reserves`: one-time template authorization; normal recruitment applies.
- `TFR_EST_reconcile_occupation`: actual patron/status selects occupation or restoration.
- `TFR_EST_reconcile_campaign`: war-stage tracking and independent main-tree repair.

HOI4 scripted effects accept scalar calls, not argument blocks or `$AMOUNT$`
parameters. Set the temporary input immediately before its helper in the same
effect context. The authoring function `amount_call` emits both statements:

```text
set_temp_variable = { var = TFR_EST_cohesion_delta value = 5 }
TFR_EST_change_cohesion = yes
```

Country-history top-level commands have separate temporary contexts. The native
QA fixture therefore wraps related statements in one `hidden_effect` block.

## Economy and military modifier foundation

`common/dynamic_modifiers/TFR_EST_country.txt` defines `TFR_EST_economy_dynamic`
and `TFR_EST_military_dynamic`. Both are restricted to EST and persist without
a time limit. They are installed during first system initialization and repaired
by the daily initialized-country hook, including during occupation/restoration.
Every field has its own persistent `TFR_EST_<category>_<modifier>_var`; all 16
start at zero. Initialization checks each variable before seeding it, so repeated
initialization or a missing modifier cannot erase saved reforms. Existing parent
modifiers, debt/inflation variables and NATO penalties remain independent.

Future authored content can adjust a backing variable and refresh immediately:

```text
add_to_variable = { TFR_EST_economy_production_speed_buildings_factor_var = 0.03 }
TFR_EST_refresh_country_modifiers = yes
```

Modifier factors use native units: `0.03` is three percentage points. Reductions
to training time, attrition or supply consumption normally use negative values.
Rewards must retain their own once-only guards; the refresh helper grants no
additional values and does not reapply an already present modifier.

The new opening policy rewards adjust these containers directly, with persistent
once-only completion guards. The later programmes retain their current ideas;
their bonuses are not copied into the containers, avoiding double counting.

## Compact opening and crisis

`TFR_EST_opening.txt` contains eleven shared focuses imported by the single main
tree. Eight older generic introduction nodes were removed; later prerequisites
now lead from `TFR_EST_opening_marriage_referendum`. All opening costs are five
focus points (35 days). The first ten require the actual original coalition,
Centre PM and mandate, 51 cabinet seats and no caretaker status.

`TFR_EST_can_start_referendum` requires each of the ten native completion records
or its earned persistent marker, conjunctively. Markers preserve legitimate
completion when an occupation tree handoff removes native IDs. December 24 is
the earliest start; even 14 banked days leave January 12–13 inside the focus.
The final uses `cancelable=no`, `cancel_if_invalid=no`, `continue_if_invalid=yes`.

Its selection effect sets `TFR_EST_referendum_active`. Daily reconciliation
queues the investigation from January 12 and the response one day after delivery.
The date is a lower bound: late selection catches up during the active focus.
Short-lived queue flags recover undelivered events after occupation, while
persistent presented flags prevent duplicates when a player leaves a notice open.
Choice helpers and completion rewards have separate once-only guards.

Resignation keeps Ratas caretaker, sets a 13-day negotiation counter and blocks
ordinary/AI reconfirmation. The successor event nominates Reform through explicit
story authorization, adds Centre and confirms only with valid confidence. The
original assembly supplies 60 seats. Unusual insufficient-confidence states can
complete their authorized handover through parliament; daily repair clears the
pending transition after confirmation. No tree swap occurs during the focus.

`TFR_EST_opening_complete` requires both referendum completion and the crisis
choice. All later nodes have `allow_branch` gates; party nodes also check PM and
caretaker status, and war/restoration nodes retain their stage gates. The guarded
`TFR_EST_refresh_focus_visibility` signature tracks stage, PM, opening, occupation,
caretaker, war state, both seen-war flags, restoration and Parempoolsed formation.
It refreshes the cached layout only when these inputs change. Availability keeps
ordinary 51-seat checks while essential war/restoration content has its own gates.

## Lifecycle and persistence

`TFR_EST_initialized` is set before parliament initialization. Parent startup
finishes before the day-one initialization event. A single root weekly hook runs
meter drift, parliament, normal content, and occupation weekly maintenance.
A daily hook reconciles occupation before the independent campaign tree.

Campaign stages: 0 opening, 1 first war, 2 interwar/reconstruction, 3 second war,
4 post-second-war. Seen-war flags persist even if parent global flags are cleared.
Occupation controls tree selection and suspends normal cabinet/election mechanics.

Each focus has a persistent completion flag and a bypass check. This is necessary
because `keep_completed` alone does not retain unrelated focus IDs across a
normal-to-occupation tree swap. Physical investments and political reforms remain
one-time. Occupation generations distinguish new patrons and actual subsequent
war settlements; war-to-peace stage changes alone do not create a new mandate.
New generations reset only
the choices that belong to a fresh occupation mandate.

Repairs do not repeat initialization, transfer territory weekly, restore enemy
control, or issue rewards. Only the actual protected settlement sets the anchor
controller. Parent delayed generic-tree changes can be repaired without preventing
combat capture in a later war.

## Compatibility boundaries

The authoritative starting political model replaces Estonia's history leadership,
while inherited technologies, influence, economic ideas, NATO/EU initialization
and DLC branches remain intact. Registration in `has_content_tag` prevents the
parent AI disable behavior at startup.

The peace patch guards annexation before execution. Other countries retain the
original annex action. Estonia keeps state 13, while the original recipient gets
the remaining intended lands. Broad country/state loops are checked as well as
literal `target = EST` statements. Russian pacification gates recognize occupied
Estonia and evaluate only territory actually owned by Russia.

Converted annexations preserve the original `transfer_troops` choice: default
or yes transfers the old army to the intended annex recipient; no destroys its
land divisions without a refund. Navy, air wings, equipment stockpiles and fuel
transfer to that recipient. Cleanup runs once per occupation generation, so a
repeated peace callback cannot seize newly raised occupation forces. Existing
puppet outcomes keep their original military behavior.

The custom occupation autonomy level cannot be downgraded or integrated normally.
Explicit scripted annexation protection is still required. Debug map operations,
arbitrary console commands and other mods are outside the audited guarantee.

The parent GDP/debt/inflation and membership effects are reused. Budget values in
the authoring source are in millions, not the parent's billion-unit debt amounts.
NATO restrictions are lifted for prepared Estonia only, guarded once per campaign.

## Assets and validation

References use effective TFR/base-game sprites and portraits. Same-relative-path
TFR interface files shadow vanilla; use the effective catalog when selecting icons.
English localization is UTF-8 with BOM. New leaders currently reuse generic portraits.

The structural validator catches syntax, duplicate IDs, missing references,
localization, sprites and focus prerequisite cycles. The limited test runtime
executes actual arithmetic/control-flow scripts using exact rational arithmetic;
it does not emulate the game engine, combat, UI, scheduler or fixed-point rounding.
Engine startup and natural campaigns provide the remaining validation layers.
